Who We Are

Compassionate Care You Can Trust

Faithful Care Haven is a residential care provider serving children and youth from infancy to 18 years of age. Our home provides structure without rigidity, support without judgment, and guidance that promotes independence and personal growth.

We aim to create a family-like environment where consistency, trust, and healthy relationships are the foundation of everything we do — because every child deserves more than a placement; they deserve a home.

The plan of care process

From admission to a confident transition.

Initial Assessment (24–72 hours)

We review placement goals, physical and mental health, education, culture, and family connections.

Individualized Goals

Measurable goals are set together with the child, their CAS worker, family (where appropriate), and other providers.

Service Delivery

Daily supervision, structured routines, trauma-informed interventions, and educational advocacy — delivered consistently.

Monitoring & Review

Daily case notes, monthly progress reviews, and ongoing collaboration with CAS and service providers.

Transition & Discharge Planning

Reunification, foster or kinship placement, independent living, or transfer — planned from day one for a smooth transition.
