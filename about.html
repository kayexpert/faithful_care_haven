About Faithful Care Haven

Founded on one belief : a placement should feel like a home.

We provide residential care for children and youth who need safety, stability, guidance, and meaningful relationships during difficult periods.

Our Mission

Creating a Safe Haven for Every Child

To provide a safe, supportive, and structured residential environment where children and youth can heal, develop life skills, and achieve positive outcomes through individualized and trauma-informed care.

We work collaboratively with Children's Aid Societies, families, schools, and community partners to ensure that each child receives consistent support aligned with their care plan and best interests.

100% Commitment

Every child receives dedicated, compassionate care backed by our full commitment to quality and safety.

Our Features

  • Individualized Care Plans
  • Licensed & Trained Staff
  • 5-Star Quality Standards
Our Commitment

Every Child's Wellbeing is Our Priority

At Faithful Care Haven Corporation, the safety, dignity, and well-being of every child is our highest calling. We are dedicated to living out these values every day.

Family First Care

We treat every child like our own family — with love, respect, care, dignity, compassion, and kindness woven into every interaction.

Trust & Reliability

Building confidence through professional, dependable, and trusted care that children, families, and partners can count on.

Joyful Daily Living

Enriching lives with positive experiences, meaningful companionship, and enjoyable structured activities that bring joy and belonging.

Expert Caregivers

Providing care with dedication, expertise, and compassionate professionals trained to support every child's unique journey.

Standards

Compliant with Ontario child welfare standards — in every home we operate.

Documentation, incident reporting, risk management, and continuous quality improvement guide everything we do — so every child receives consistent, accountable, and compassionate care.

Talk to Our Team

Documentation Protocols

Accurate daily case notes, progress records, and audit-ready files for every placement, updated in real time by trained staff.

Incident Reporting

Structured reporting, root-cause analysis, and timely CAS notifications ensure transparency and continual learning.

Risk Management

Proactive safety assessments, mitigation plans, and regular environment audits keep every home secure and predictable.

Quality Improvement

Ongoing reviews, staff feedback, and outcome tracking refine our programs and raise the standard of care month over month.

Our team

Trained, screened, and consistently supported.

Every staff member completes Vulnerable Sector Screening, First Aid & CPR certification, child protection training, trauma-informed care training, crisis prevention training, medication training, and ongoing professional development.
