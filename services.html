Our Services

Comprehensive Care Solutions

Safe, trauma-informed residential care, clinical supports, and family-centered development services — backed by 100% commitment.

Residential Care

Safe, structured homes with caring daily support.

Our residential program provides children and youth with stability, predictable routines, and a warm home environment. Staff are present all day to offer guidance, supervision, and emotional safety.

  • Family-style residences with private or semi-private rooms
  • Daily structure, hygiene support, and nourishing meals
  • Positive routines for homework, recreation, and life skills
Individualized Planning

Personal care plans designed with every child in mind.

We develop individualized plans in partnership with children, families, and referral partners. Each plan is focused on practical goals, therapeutic supports, and measurable progress.

  • Collaborative plan development with CAS and caregivers
  • Health, education, and behaviour supports coordinated together
  • Regular review to keep care responsive and strength-based
Trauma-Informed Care

Evidence-based support that promotes healing and resilience.

Our practitioners use trauma-informed methods to help children regulate, build trust, and feel understood. The focus is on safety, consistency, and positive behavioural connections.

  • Regulation strategies grounded in trauma awareness
  • Support for emotional development and healthy relationships
  • Strength-based behaviour guidance built on respect
Who We Serve

Children & Youth in Need of Care

Faithful Care Haven Corporation provides care for children and youth requiring short-term or long-term residential placement, including those who may have experienced:

  • ✓
    Trauma and adverse childhood experiences
  • ✓
    Instability or disruption in living situations
  • ✓
    Disruptions in previous care placements
  • ✓
    Emotional and behavioural challenges requiring support
  • ✓
    Need for structured, therapeutic environments

We welcome placement inquiries from Children's Aid Societies and community partners.
