Service 01
Residential & Daily Living

A warm, structured home — with 24/7 supervised care.

Children and youth thrive in environments that combine safety with dignity. Our residential home provides consistent, family-style routines, clean and comfortable accommodations, nutritious meals, and trained staff on-site around the clock — so no child is ever without support when they need it most.

24/7 On-site supervision

What's included

Safe Accommodation & Utilities

Private and semi-private rooms in a family-style residential setting with all utilities, furnishings, linens, and household supplies provided.

Nutritious Meals & Snacks

Three balanced meals plus snacks every day, reflecting cultural, dietary, and allergen requirements — with opportunities for children to help with meal prep and menu planning.

Personal Care Assistance

Support with activities of daily living — hygiene, grooming, laundry, medication reminders, and household routines — tailored to each child's age and abilities.

24-Hour Supervised Care

Round-the-clock awake staffing with regular wellness checks, structured bedtimes and wake-up routines, and an on-call chain of support for every shift.

Recreation & Community Activities

Outings, sports, arts, hobbies, and community events — designed to build social skills, self-esteem, and positive memories outside the home.

Daily Structure

Predictable, calm routines — wake-up, meals, school, homework time, recreation, family contact, and bedtime — help children feel grounded and secure.

Placement Types

We welcome temporary, emergency, respite, transitional, and long-term placements — each reviewed individually to ensure the right fit and staffing capacity.

Transportation

Safe, scheduled transport for school, medical appointments, community programs, and approved family visitation — coordinated by trained staff.

Service 02
Clinical & Behavioral Care

Trauma-informed support for every healing journey.

Many children enter care carrying experiences of trauma, family disruption, or emotional distress. Our clinical and behavioral services meet them where they are — with individualized plans, trauma-informed interventions, and coordinated access to medical, dental, and mental health professionals.

How we support behavioral & clinical needs

Individualized Care Plans — every child has a written Plan of Care developed within 24–72 hours of admission.
Trauma-Informed Interventions — de-escalation, regulation strategies, sensory supports, and calm-space protocols rather than punitive responses.
Medical & Dental Coordination — appointment booking, medication management, health monitoring, and communication with pediatricians, dentists, and other specialists.
Mental Health Collaboration — ongoing liaison with CAS, psychologists, psychiatrists, therapists, and counselors as part of the care team.
Crisis Prevention — all staff trained in non-violent crisis intervention and de-escalation, with clear reporting and post-incident review.
Cultural & Identity Responsiveness — the Plan of Care honors each child's culture, spirituality, traditions, and personal identity.
Plan of Care within 24–72h
The Initial Assessment

Within 24–72 hours, a comprehensive review covers

🩺

Physical Health

Medical history, current medications, allergies, immunizations, and any ongoing or acute physical health needs.

🧠

Mental & Emotional

Trauma history, emotional regulation patterns, coping strategies, and links to existing mental health providers.

🎒

Educational Status

Current school, grade, attendance, learning needs, IEPs or special education, and school transition coordination.

👥

Social & Behavioral

Peer and adult relationship patterns, triggers, behavioral strengths, and areas for supportive skill-building.

🌱

Cultural & Identity

Spiritual, cultural, and linguistic background — and how those important parts of identity are honored at home.

🏠

Family Connections

Approved family and kinship networks, CAS visitation arrangements, and permanency or reunification goals.

Service 03
Development & Family Support

Building skills, bonds, and a path toward tomorrow.

A residential placement is never an end in itself. Our Development & Family pillar prepares each child and youth for what comes next — strong educational foundations, independent life skills, healthy family connections (where safe and appropriate), and a transition plan built on their individual goals.

Education Coordination

Every child deserves access to quality learning. We coordinate school enrollment, attend case conferences, support homework routines, advocate for IEPs, and track attendance and learning progress alongside the school team.

  • School registration & transfer coordination
  • Homework supervision & tutoring links
  • IEP / special-ed liaison & advocacy
  • Attendance monitoring & reporting to CAS
  • Credit recovery & alternative-school pathways

Life Skills Development

From the youngest children learning self-care to older teens preparing for independent living, we deliver age-appropriate programming so every young person leaves with practical skills for life.

  • Household tasks, cooking, and laundry basics
  • Money management & budgeting skills
  • Communication & conflict-resolution practice
  • Decision-making & healthy boundaries
  • Employment readiness & résumé help for youth
  • Community safety & street-proofing

Family Engagement

Where it is safe and approved by the Children's Aid Society, we actively support family and kinship connections — including supervised visits, phone/video calls, and collaborative permanency planning.

  • CAS-approved visitation facilitation
  • Family contact & communication scheduling
  • Cultural & ceremonial connection supports
  • Reunification planning partnerships
  • Transition meetings with kinship / foster teams
Transition & Discharge

Planning for what comes next starts at admission.

Whether the goal is reunification with family, a move to a foster or kinship home, transition to independent living, or transfer to another service setting, our team collaborates with CAS, the child, family, and community partners to plan from day one.
