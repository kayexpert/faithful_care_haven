# FAITHFUL CARE HAVEN

# Residential Placement Services Proposal

**Executive Summary**

Faithful Care Haven was founded on a simple belief: every child deserves more than a placement they deserve a home.

We provide residential care services for children and youth who require safety, stability, guidance, and meaningful relationships during difficult periods in their lives. Our approach goes beyond supervision and basic care. We focus on helping children rebuild trust, develop confidence, strengthen life skills, and prepare for a successful future.

We recognize that many children entering care have experienced trauma, instability, family disruption, or emotional and social challenges. Our role is to create a safe and supportive environment where young people feel valued, respected, heard, and supported.

**Who We Are**

Faithful Care Haven is a residential care provider serving children and youth from infancy to 18 years of age who require temporary, emergency, respite, transitional, or long-term placement.

Our home provides structure without rigidity, support without judgment, and guidance that promotes independence and personal growth. We aim to create a family-like environment where consistency, trust, and healthy relationships are prioritized.

**Philosophy of Care**

Children thrive when they experience:

-   Stability

-   Consistency

-   Positive relationships

-   Meaningful opportunities

-   A sense of belonging

We believe children should not be defined by their past experiences, behaviors, or challenges. Instead, we focus on strengths, resilience, and personal development.

Every child in our care is treated with dignity, respect, and compassion.

**Population Served**

Faithful Care Haven supports children and youth who may be experiencing:

-   Placement breakdowns

-   Family conflict

-   Emotional and behavioral challenges

-   Trauma-related needs

-   Mental health concerns

-   Developmental disabilities

-   Educational disruptions

-   Social adjustment difficulties

-   Independent living preparation needs

Each placement is reviewed individually to ensure appropriate fit and support capacity.

**Model of Care**

**Stability First**

We prioritize helping children feel safe, secure, and supported through consistent routines and expectations.

**Relationship-Based Care**

Staff build trusting and supportive relationships that encourage emotional growth and stability.

**Strength-Based Approach**

We focus on identifying strengths, interests, and abilities rather than solely on challenges.

**Future-Focused Development**

We support youth in building life skills for independence, including education, communication, decision-making, and employment readiness.

**Services Provided**

Faithful Care Haven offers comprehensive residential supports, including:

-   24-hour supervised residential care

-   Individualized care planning

-   Trauma-informed behavioral support

-   Personal care and daily living assistance

-   Educational support and school coordination

-   Life skills development programming

-   Family engagement and visitation support (as approved by CAS)

-   Medical, dental, and mental health coordination

-   Recreation and community participation

**Staffing and Qualifications**

Faithful Care Haven is supported by trained professionals committed to providing safe and consistent care.

All staff complete:

-   Vulnerable Sector Screening

-   First Aid and CPR certification

-   Child protection training

-   Trauma-informed care training

-   Crisis prevention training

-   Ongoing professional development

-   Medication training

Our team is committed to maintaining a structured and supportive environment for all children and youth in care.

**Safety and Accountability**

We are committed to maintaining high standards of care and accountability through:

-   Detailed documentation and daily reporting

-   Incident reporting procedures

-   Risk management and safety planning

-   Regular supervision and staff training

-   Continuous quality improvement

We operate in compliance with all applicable Ontario child welfare standards and legislation.

**Plan of Care**

At Faithful Care Haven, every child and youth admitted into our program receives an individualized Plan of Care designed to address their unique needs, strengths, goals, and circumstances. The Plan of Care serves as a roadmap for supporting the child's safety, well-being, development, and long-term success.

**Initial Assessment**

Within 24–72 hours of admission, staff will complete a comprehensive assessment that reviews:

-   Placement goals and referral information

-   Physical health and medical needs

-   Mental health and emotional well-being

-   Educational status and learning needs

-   Social and behavioral functioning

-   Cultural, spiritual, and personal identity considerations

-   Life skills and developmental needs

-   Family connections and approved visitation arrangements

**Individualized Goals**

Based on the assessment, measurable goals will be established in collaboration with the child or youth, Children's Aid Society worker, family members (where appropriate), and other service providers.

Goals may include:

-   Emotional regulation and coping skills

-   Educational achievement and school attendance

-   Positive social relationships

-   Independent living and life skills development

-   Behavioral stability

-   Mental health support and treatment participation

-   Family reunification or permanency planning

-   Community engagement and recreational participation

**Service Delivery**

The Plan of Care is implemented through a coordinated approach that includes:

-   Daily supervision and support

-   Structured routines and expectations

-   Trauma-informed interventions

-   Individual mentoring and guidance

-   Access to community resources and professional services

-   Educational advocacy and support

-   Health and wellness monitoring

-   Life skills training and preparation for independence

**Monitoring and Review**

Progress toward goals is monitored on an ongoing basis through:

-   Daily documentation and case notes

-   Regular staff observations and feedback

-   Monthly progress reviews

-   Collaboration with Children's Aid Society and service providers

-   Updates to the Plan of Care as needs change

Formal reviews will be conducted regularly to evaluate progress, identify emerging needs, and ensure services remain aligned with the child or youth's best interests.

**Transition and Discharge Planning**

Planning for successful transitions begins at admission and may include:

-   Reunification with family

-   Placement to a foster or kinship home

-   Transition to independent living

-   Transfer to another appropriate service setting

Prior to discharge, staff will work collaboratively with all involved parties to ensure continuity of care, access to resources, and a smooth transition that supports long-term stability and success.

**Commitment to Positive Outcomes**

Faithful Care Haven is committed to providing care that is child-centered, trauma-informed, culturally responsive, and outcome-focused. Through individualized planning and consistent support, we strive to help every child and youth achieve safety, stability, personal growth, and a strong foundation for the future.

**Placement Process**

Faithful Care Haven follows a structured placement process in collaboration with Children's Aid Society:

-   Review of referral and background information

-   Assessment of placement suitability

-   Confirmation of availability and staffing capacity

-   Admission and orientation to the home

-   Development of individualized care plan within 24–72 hours

**Conclusion**

Faithful Care Haven is committed to providing safe, stable, and supportive residential care for children and youth in need of placement.

Our approach is grounded in structure, consistency, and individualized support, ensuring that each young person receives care that reflects their unique needs and circumstances.

We are committed to maintaining high standards of safety, accountability, and professionalism in all aspects of service delivery.

We look forward to working in collaboration with Children's Aid Society to support positive outcomes, stability, and long-term well-being for children and youth in care.
